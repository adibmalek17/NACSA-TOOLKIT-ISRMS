EXPECTED RISK EXCEL INPUT

File: .xlsx, up to 2 MB. Worksheet name: Assessments.
Put column names in row 1 and one matching assessment row in row 2.
Use numbers, not formulas. Columns may be reordered.

NACSA profiles:
expected_c, expected_i, expected_a, expected_likelihood, expected_loss

USIM profile:
expected_c, expected_i, expected_a, expected_likelihood, expected_operations, expected_financial, expected_reputation

nacsa-3-2024 and usim-2026: whole numbers 1 to 3.
nacsa-5-2024: whole numbers 1 to 5.

Optional matching columns: asset_code, profile.
If present and nonblank they must match the selected asset/profile.
Keep one matching row; multiple matching rows are rejected.

Expected-only upload: use the columns above in the Expected Risk Scoring upload.
Combined upload: add the expected_ columns to your existing assessment workbook and use the top Upload Excel control.
Do not populate loss fields for USIM, or operations/financial/reputation fields for NACSA.
Old assessment workbooks without expected columns still work and leave expected risk unassessed.
